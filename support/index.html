Support

Help for Legaryn: Mythic Legends on iPhone and Android.

Contact us

Email us and a real person will reply, usually within 2 business days. Please include your Warlord name and, for purchase problems, the date of the purchase.

support@legaryn.com Discord

A purchase didn't arrive

  1. Fully close Legaryn and open it again.
  2. Open the Sanctum (store) and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom.
  3. Still missing? Email us with the date and your App Store or Google Play receipt.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play Store order history.

Save my progress or move to a new phone

Open Settings and link your Google or Apple account. Sign in with the same account on your new phone and your Warlord comes with you.

Report or block a player

In any chat, press and hold a player's name to mute, block or report them. Reports come straight to us and are reviewed. Hateful words are blocked automatically, and swearing is hidden.

Delete my account

Open Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and game data. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Summon odds

The exact odds and guarantees for every summon are listed in the game on the Summon screen under Odds & Guarantees.
