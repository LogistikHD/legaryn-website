Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

Legaryn ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Legaryn mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Data Collection and Authentication

To provide game progression, cross-device syncing, and multiplayer interaction, we process data using the following methods:

  • Anonymous Profiles: When you first open the App, a unique, anonymous user identifier (UID) is generated. This profile contains no personal identifiers and tracks local gameplay progress.
  • Third-Party Authentication (Google & Apple): You may choose to bind your anonymous profile to a Google or Apple account. When you authenticate using these services, we collect your unique provider user ID, email address, and profile name to securely save your progress and verify your identity for social interactions. If you choose Sign in with Apple, you may also opt to hide your private email address using Apple's private relay system.
  • Social Interaction Data: If you interact with other users within the App, data related to those interactions (such as public profile names or scores) may be displayed to other users.

2. Infrastructure and Data Processing (Supabase)

Our app backend is powered by Supabase. All authentication tokens, database records, and user progression states are securely stored and processed on Supabase cloud infrastructure. Your data is handled in strict accordance with infrastructure security standards and is never sold to third parties.

3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. Apple App Store rules require that you can delete your data at any time. If you wish to permanently delete your account, saved game progress, and all associated authenticated data, you may do so through the settings menu inside the App or by contacting us directly.
