Every Legend Became Real

In the Hollow Realm, fear has weight. Every tale told long enough took a body. Now a storm called the Hollow Tide swallows a little more of the land each night, and only ground lit by a Bloodstone survives.

I

The Last City

You wake in Ashmoor as the bells ring. Fog on the walls, the Tide at the gates. Bind your first beast before the dark comes in.

II

The Warlords

You carry Legaryn, the ancient bond that lets a mortal bind beasts. Few still do. Fewer survive it.

III

The Blood Pact

There are not enough Bloodstones for everyone. Swear an oath with other Warlords, feed your stones, and hold your ground.

The Price
